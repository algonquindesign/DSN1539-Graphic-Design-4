Assignment 2 - 20%
Stationery (print and digital)

Overview
You will design and present a stationery package for your client.
The stationery submission will include letterhead, business card and envelope.

Letterhead
Size: 8.5" width x 11" height, Bleed if desired.
Content: Entire Logo, address and contact information
Stock: matte stock

Business Card
Size - Folded if folded or flat if not folded: standard card 3.5" width x 2" height or 2" width x 3.5" height, Bleed if desired
You can have it fold out or not.
Content: logo, a name (could use your own) and position, address and contact information
Stock: gloss or matte stock.
Special Instructions: can be printed on both sides or have a special die-cut or fold if desired

Envelope
Size: standard number 10 business envelope (4 1/8" x 9 1/2")
Content: logo and return address only
Stock: matte stock
The envelope does not have to be produced from a production perspective. It can be mocked-up flat and cut out. The front and back should be the standard #10 business envelope size. When you are displaying it make sure to include the flat of the envelope to show your selection choice.

Please also visit the Canada Post website to determine restricted areas for the envelope.

NOTE: The three pieces when presented together should have a family look as discussed in class.
