Assignment 3 - Milestones +

Logo Standard Branding Guide - 35%

OVERVIEW

For this assignment, you will be creating a logo standard branding guide to accompany your logo. This guide is for the client/company or any person who will be working with the logo. It helps your client know what, when, where and how to use the logo design. It is an informational guide that provides insight, instructions and guidelines.

  • You will build the file in InDesign. It is recommended to sketch out your layout intent to ensure you have a good plan prior to starting your InDesign file.
  • There will be a grid involved in your design to ensure a good flow throughout.
  • The book page count will be multiples of 4, and there will be no less than 16 pages (not including cover, which will be another 4 pages). There can be more than 16 pages, but it has to be in multiples of four. You will determine the number of pages when you sketch out your layout.
  • Bleeds are permitted.
  • Think spreads - page on the left and page on the right-hand side.
  • Even numbers to the left and odd numbers to the right.

ASSIGNMENT SPECIFICATIONS &
SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
Failure to submit final file submission will result in an automatic fail. You will forfeit your milestone marks.

The table of contents should link to each section.
