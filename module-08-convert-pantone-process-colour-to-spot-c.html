Weekly Schedule · Assignment 1: Logo Re-design

Your logo will be created in Pantone Spot Colours. No tonal value or gradations in the logo colour.

Normally when we use SPOT COLOURS designers use PANTONE SOLID SPOT COLOURS that they select from Pantone Swatch Books. These colours are only used on one, two or three colour printing jobs and are most commonly used in logo design. However Adobe has taken out all the Pantone Colour Swatches from their software. We will create SPOT colours from CMYK swatches.

Open a new page in Illustrator 8.5x11 landscape or portrait. Make sure the colour mode is CMYK.

Image not in the export: Pantone Colour SwatchesPageSetup.jpg

Find the Pantone colours that you want from this link:

https://vector-conversions.com/vector/pantone_color_swatchbook.pdf

Choose your colours from this catalogue. Take a screen shot of the colours, bring image into illustrator and eye drop the colour to a blank box.

Image not in the export: Spot.jpg

This colour box will be CMYK so then you will convert your CMYK colours to a spot colours for all your colours

Open Swatches - click on + (add swatch) or as shown below click on three bars to the left to create new swatch. Colour type will be SPOT COLOUR. You can name your new colour to the Pantone Colour Code from the catalogue - change from CMYK to Spot Colour and click OK - you now have a new SPOT colour

Image not in the export: Spot3.jpg

Image not in the export: Spot21.jpg

Image not in the export: Spot1.jpg

You can check your overall colours by using the Separation Preview. Go to Window and drop down to Separations Preview. You will see the CMYK colour as well as your new colour swatches.

NOTE: Instructor will be checking your illustrator ai files
