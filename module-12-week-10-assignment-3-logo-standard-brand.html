Logo Standard Branding Guide - 35%

OVERVIEW

For this assignment, you will be creating a logo standard branding guide to accompany your logo. This guide is for the client/company or any person who will be working with the logo. It helps your client know what, when, where and how to use the logo design. It is an informational guide that provides insight, instructions and guidelines.

  • You will build the file in InDesign. It is recommended to sketch out your layout intent to ensure you have a good plan prior to starting your InDesign file.
  • There will be a grid involved in your design to ensure a good flow throughout.
  • The book page count will be multiples of 4, and there will be no less than 16 pages (not including cover, which will be another 4 pages). There can be more than 16 pages, but it has to be in multiples of four. You will determine the number of pages when you sketch out your layout.
  • This publication will be considered separate cover which means if the publication was really printed then the cover, inside front cover and inside back cover and back cover would be printed on a different heavier stock than the inside pages. Normally this would be set up as separate artwork but for this project we will just set it up all in one file. When we have a separate cover then the page numbering on the inside would start as the table of contents on p1.
  • Bleeds are permitted.
  • Think spreads - page on the left and page on the right-hand side.
  • Even numbers to the left and odd numbers to the right.

Digital-PDF spreads uploaded to Brirightspace in week 10 of the following sections:

  • Cover - Logo + Title + Date it was created & Back Cover
  • Inside cover & back inside cover - Can be used as a design element, but most times it is left to stock colour due to cost reasons.
  • Contents page - do not include a table of contents page in the table of contents. Example: Table of Contents......page 1 - Start numbering on page 3
  • Signature - Story behind the logo, provides insight into the structure of the logo and how it works with the strategy of the company and it's the future direction. Write an explanation in this area.
  • Logo artwork - How and how not to reproduce the logo. Provide file format information and which file is best to use in what circumstance - Print vs Web/Digital.
  • Logo colours - Pantone Colour, Black & White and Reverse versions of your logo.
    Colour breakdown - Show colour swatch with Pantone number, CMYK, RGB and Hex or websafe colour. Please remember the differences between Coated and Uncoated Pantone colours. Can specify uses if you desire. If you feel you want restrictions you can provide examples.
  • Link to how to convert RGB or CMYK to HEX https://imagecolorpicker.com/color-code/2596be

WEEK 12 - Second section of the guide
Due at the beginning of class.
Digital-PDF spreads uploaded to Brirightspace in week 11 of the following sections:

  • Backgrounds and contrast - Explain what version can be used and where. Acceptable and not acceptable visual examples. Don't forget about the photographs.
  • Typeface
    Logo Typeface - List your font type name(s) and all the font family selection used in Logo (Bold. Medium, Thin...etc). Show the font in action. Sometimes using the logo and pointing to the area where the typeface is used is helpful.
    Please include the entire alphabet or "The fast fox jumps of the lazy dog", plus numbers.
    Print Typeface: This can be your logo typeface or another font(s) that you would recommend that should be used in conjunction with your logo (Example for headings or copy/text). Basically what font typeface would you want to see used in marketing materials. The demonstration of this selection in the same manner as the Logo typeface.
  • NOTE: It is also helpful to provide a generic font type (on most computers) that can be used in written materials that compliments your logo font choice. This is for administrative staff for stationery.
  • Web Typeface: This is what web save font is to be used on your website. Google fonts. Same way of demonstrating as logo typeface.
  • Alternate logo design (if applicable - horizontal or alternate vertical version if you created one and or/wordmark if applicable.)
  • Digital online presence - Social media icon images (Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter - symbols/ICONS) - how you would utilize them in a web environment or print. Example: button/links. Please make sure to adhere to the Social Media restrictions for their brand, while customizing them for your branding.
    Please include Favicon, Facebook profile image, Wallpaper. Finally a website mock-up of the home page.
  • Buffer zone - A safe area that nothing should encroach upon. This is shown by using an element of the logo (At the size it appears. Do not resize.). This is a must. Make sure to include both versions (vertical and horizontal) of your logo. You can also incorporate your icon/pictorial if you so wish.
  • Logo reduction - How small can the logo be reduced. Provide visuals.

WEEK 13 - Third section of the guide
Due at the beginning of class.
Digital-PDF spreads uploaded to Brirightspace in week 12 of the following sections:

  • Symbol only - Icon or symbol of the logo. Explain how and when it can be used as a whole or pieces. Can it be broken apart and parts of it used for the design in marketing materials. If yes, how and when.
  • Graphic treatment (watermark) - How the symbol or icon can be used as a graphical element. Provide a visual representation. Do not give only a % for tonal value, also provide an example, so they have a visual to reference. Example - when you use the icon in the background and shade it back. Do this for both the colour and BW.
  • Stationery - Letter Head + Business Card + Envelope. After laying these out you can also use a PSD file to show these items produced. There are plenty online to use. Try to specify the paper choice and weight etc for letterhead and business card but not necessary for this project.
  • Applications - Logo in use on promotional signage and items (front of the store, cups, hats. Use Photoshop to superimpose your logo on items and in areas. Photography - how all photography should be handed from a look branding perspective. (You CAN use stock photography for this and alter it if need be).
  • Contact info (Company and if you like a designer) - You can place this on cover/back cover or any way you see fit.
