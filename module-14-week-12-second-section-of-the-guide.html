Weekly Schedule · Assignment 3: Logo Standards Guide

WEEK 12 - Second section of the guide
Due at the beginning of class.
Digital-PDF spreads uploaded to Brirightspace in week 12 of the following sections:

  • Backgrounds and contrast - Explain what version can be used and where. Acceptable and not acceptable visual examples. Don't forget about the photographs.
  • Typeface
    Logo Typeface - List your font type name(s) and all the font family selection used in Logo (Bold. Medium, Thin...etc). Show the font in action. Sometimes using the logo and pointing to the area where the typeface is used is helpful.
    Please include the entire alphabet or "The fast fox jumps of the lazy dog", plus numbers.
    Print Typeface: This can be your logo typeface or another font(s) that you would recommend that should be used in conjunction with your logo (Example for headings or copy/text). Basically what font typeface would you want to see used in marketing materials. The demonstration of this selection in the same manner as the Logo typeface.
  • NOTE: It is also helpful to provide a generic font type (on most computers) that can be used in written materials that compliments your logo font choice. This is for administrative staff for stationary.
  • Web Typeface: This is what web save font is to be used on your website. Google fonts. Same way of demonstrating as logo typeface.
  • Alternate logo design (if applicable - horizontal or alternate vertical version if you created one and or/wordmark if applicable.)
  • Digital online presence - Social media icon images (Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter - symbols/ICONS) - how you would utilize them in a web environment or print. Example: button/links. Please make sure to adhere to the Social Media restrictions for their brand, while customizing them for your branding.
    Please include Favicon, Facebook profile image, Wallpaper. Finally a website mock-up of the home page.
  • Buffer zone - A safe area that nothing should encroach upon. This is shown by using an element of the logo (At the size it appears. Do not resize.). This is a must. Make sure to include both versions (vertical and horizontal) of your logo. You can also incorporate your icon/pictorial if you so wish.
