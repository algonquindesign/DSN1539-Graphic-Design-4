Course description

In depth research and concept development techniques lead to a more successful resolution of the design challenge at hand. Students design a corporate identity package, including a central graphic identifier, identity manifestations and a corporate identity standards manual. Students continue to work in a four-stage process similar to that used in industry: research, creative development, execution and presentation. The main focus is on concepts and the creative process.

Course learning outcomes

  1. Present and explain your design concepts.
  2. Apply completed research and creative problem solving to complete design.
  3. Produce and present high quality comprehensives.
  4. Establish a design style suitable for a subject.
  5. Create and apply a brand strategy that fulfills the client's needs for both digital and print.
